About StoryNest

What is this?

StoryNest is a small storybook website inspired by Wattpad. It has 30 original single-chapter stories across 30 different genres.

How to use it

Go to the Library, search or filter by genre, then press Read Story on any book. On a story page you can like the story, show or hide the synopsis, and follow your reading progress at the top of the screen.

Features

Dark/light mode, like button, show/hide synopsis, reading progress bar, and search and genre filter.
