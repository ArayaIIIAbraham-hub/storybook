Fourth Quarter Miracle

by Troy Mason

Synopsis: A benchwarmer gets one shot to win the championship.

Chapter 1: Ten Seconds Left

Down by two with ten seconds left, Coach pointed at the bench. 'Ramos, you're in.' Ramos had not played all season.

His knees shook as he took the inbound pass. The crowd roared, but all he could hear was his own heartbeat.

He dribbled, spun, and let go. The ball hung in the air for what felt like a very long time.
