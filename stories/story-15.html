The Girl Who Spoke to Shadows

by Lina Mercer

Synopsis: Mara can hear what shadows say, and tonight they are afraid.

Chapter 1: What the Dark Knows

Mara never told anyone that shadows talk. They mostly gossiped about people's secrets and the weather.

But tonight every shadow in her room was whispering the same word, over and over: 'Hide.'

Then the streetlight outside went out, and her own shadow tugged her sleeve and pointed to the closet.
