The Frog Who Wrote Wishes

by Penny Aldous

Synopsis: A talking frog trades wishes for rhymes.

Chapter 1: One Rhyme per Wish

By the pond at the edge of the kingdom lived a frog who could grant wishes. There was one rule: you had to ask in rhyme.

Princess Wren tried, 'I wish for a hat, and also a cat.' The frog sighed but gave her both.

The next day she came back with a much bigger wish, and a much worse rhyme.
