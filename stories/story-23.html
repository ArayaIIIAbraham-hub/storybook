Summer I Turned Sixteen

by Hannah Cole

Synopsis: One summer of bad decisions, new friends, and growing up.

Chapter 1: The Bus to Nowhere

On her sixteenth birthday, Sam got a bus pass and a lecture. She decided to use the pass first.

She rode to the end of the line and found a tiny beach town with one ice cream shop and no rules.

By sunset, she had a job offer, a stray dog, and the strange feeling that her life had finally started.
