Dust and Redemption

by Clay Dalton

Synopsis: A retired gunslinger is dragged back for one last ride.

Chapter 1: A Stranger at Noon

Jed Calloway had hung up his guns eleven years ago. At noon, a stranger on a gray horse rode into his dusty town.

'They took the Miller girl,' the stranger said. 'You are the only one who knows the canyon.'

Jed took down the dusty holster from the wall. The leather still fit, and that bothered him more than anything.
