Letters from the Front

by Walter Brandt

Synopsis: A soldier's letters home reveal what the headlines never did.

Chapter 1: Dear Mother

Dear Mother, the mud here is deeper than the ocean, and the days are longer than winters at home.

We share one candle between six men. Tonight Peter read us his sister's letter, and we all pretended not to cry.

Do not worry. I have your scarf. Tell Papa I kept my promise to write every week.

— End of Chapter —
