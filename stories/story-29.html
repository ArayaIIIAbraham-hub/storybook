Crimson Tide Crew

by Barnaby Teach

Synopsis: A cabin boy accidentally becomes captain of a cursed ship.

Chapter 1: Mutiny by Mistake

Pip only wanted to polish the brass. Unfortunately, he walked in just as the crew voted out the captain.

'He's the only one who didn't vote,' said the cook. 'He's captain now.' Pip dropped his rag.

The ship's figurehead winked at him. Pip decided right then that this was going to be a very strange career.
