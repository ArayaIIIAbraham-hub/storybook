Letters to Marigold

by Elena Rossi

Synopsis: Two strangers share one mailbox and start writing to each other.

Chapter 1: The Wrong Address

Noah had lived in Apartment 4B for a month before he found the first letter. It was addressed to Marigold, who had moved out long ago.

He should have returned it. Instead, he wrote back, 'Marigold is gone, but I read your letter and I liked it.'

Three days later there was a reply in the mailbox, written in green ink: 'Then you will do.'
