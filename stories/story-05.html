Whispers in Hollow Hall

by Morgan Blackwood

Synopsis: A family moves into a house that answers back.

Chapter 1: Something in the Walls

The Carters loved Hollow Hall at first sight. It was cheap, big, and quiet, almost too quiet.

On the first night, seven-year-old Ben told his mother that the walls were whispering his name.

She laughed and kissed his forehead. Then, from behind the wallpaper, a soft voice said, 'Goodnight, Ben.'
