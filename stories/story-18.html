The Midnight Librarian

by Ivy Sorrel

Synopsis: The town library opens only at midnight, and only for the dead.

Chapter 1: Overdue

Cassie took the night shift because the pay was good. Nobody mentioned that the patrons would be dead.

A pale gentleman returned a book from 1893 and asked, politely, whether there was a fine.

'We waive it,' Cassie said, shaking, and stamped the card. He smiled and tipped a hat that wasn't there.
