The Vanishing Vault

by Dorian Steele

Synopsis: A perfect heist goes wrong when the vault is already empty.

Chapter 1: Nothing Inside

The crew spent six months planning. They cut the power, cracked the lock, and swung open the vault door.

Empty. Not a single bill, not a single bar. Only a sticky note in the middle: 'Too slow.'

Rico read it twice. 'Someone beat us to it,' he muttered, 'and they have a sense of humor.'
